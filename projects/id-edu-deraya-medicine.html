Deraya Faculty of Medicine

Deraya University, New Minya

The Faculty of Medicine at Deraya University occupies a 23,000 m² campus in New Minya — a basement, ground floor, and two upper levels with a partial roof for services. The basement houses parking and technical infrastructure; the upper floors accommodate lecture halls, classrooms, laboratories, and staff offices across a generously scaled academic plan.

The interior character is defined by expansive atriums: double and triple-height voids that visually connect the floors and flood the building with natural light. Light-coloured flooring, wood panelling, and skylights reinforce the bright, open atmosphere. Sleek architectural details and a minimalist palette give the faculty a contemporary feel appropriate to a leading medical institution.

The faculty also incorporates a library, a faculty council chamber, and an array of specialised learning environments — all unified by the same restrained design language.
