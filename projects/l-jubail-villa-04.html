Jubail Villa 04

265 m²

A 265 m² private villa garden with marble walkways.

Jubail Villa 04 is a 265 m² private villa landscape design featuring a local marble walkway that winds through the garden, connecting the entrance to a series of shaded terraces. The entrance sequence is punctuated by decorative stepping stones that create rhythm and visual interest underfoot.

The terraces provide a series of intimate outdoor living zones, each planted with a variety of seasonal flowers and ornamental plants selected for colour, texture, and year-round interest. The car parking area is finished in basalt for durability and contrast. Adjacent to the house, a pergola-shaded bar area provides the perfect setting for outdoor entertaining — a refined private retreat designed with the same attention to material quality and spatial experience that defines all of Inspire's work.
