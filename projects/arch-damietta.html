New Damietta Residence

New Damietta, Egypt

A four-story classical revival residential building with basement, ground floor, first and second floors, and roof. Constructed of red brick with white limestone trim, the front facade features a portico with two columns supporting a classical arch.

The building is divided into 5 units of different sizes, each with its own entrance and private balcony. Units are spacious and well-lit with high ceilings and large windows. The building includes a spacious garden with a variety of trees and flowers — a great place for residents to relax outdoors.
