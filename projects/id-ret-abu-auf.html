Abu Auf

Cairo Festival City, New Cairo

The Abu Auf nuts, chocolate, and coffee outlet at Cairo Festival City is designed in a warm modern style. Cosy wood and off-white tones form the base palette, with orange — drawn from the brand identity — used as an accent colour that adds energy and warmth to the space.

Light marble polished flooring contributes a sense of freshness and cleanliness, while the modern ceiling design adds sophistication. The overall result is a stylish, inviting retail environment perfectly suited for a premium artisan food brand.
