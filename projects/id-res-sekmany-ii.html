Sekmany Residence II

New Cairo

This stunning contemporary classical apartment in the same New Cairo residence as Sekmany I features a spacious reception and dining area with high ceilings, a connected living area, kitchen, guest toilet, master bedroom, and two bedrooms sharing a bathroom. The warm colour scheme uses polished light-coloured marble with a simple geometric pattern at the vestibule, mixed with dark brown for contrast.

Creative wall mirrors and claddings add layers of luxury and sophistication. Like its companion residence, the building offers a pool, gym, and playground within a quiet and secure family setting.
