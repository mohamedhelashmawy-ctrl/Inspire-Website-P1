Jubail Villa II

Jubail, Sixth of October

This contemporary villa in Sixth of October spans three levels with a modern and practical character throughout. The ground floor features a spacious entrance lobby and reception, dining room, guest toilet, kitchen, and maid's room. Reception walls carry intricate colourful artworks and wall recesses with steel partitions, while openings are clad with wooden architraves. Wide ground-floor openings open to a lovely garden and swimming pool.

The first floor has a wide corridor connecting a master bedroom with two en-suite bedrooms. The penthouse provides an additional living room with private toilet and a large roof terrace. Floors are marble in the lobby, stairs, and corridors, with modern wood planks in the main rooms. All ceilings are sleek with indirect lighting.
