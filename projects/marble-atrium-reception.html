Marble Atrium Reception

Deraya University Hospital, New Minya

A dignified entrance for Deraya University Hospital.

The marble atrium reception of Deraya University Hospital was designed to create a dignified, calming first impression for patients and visitors — an entrance experience that communicates the hospital's commitment to quality care through architectural excellence.

The use of marble as the primary material brings a sense of permanence and prestige, while the double-height atrium volume and carefully placed natural light sources create an uplifting rather than clinical atmosphere. Custom millwork and bespoke reception furniture complete the refined interior.
