Stove Restaurant — New Cairo

New Cairo

The Stove New Cairo restaurant is a refined, multi-level dining environment built around a sleek grey palette with gold accents in the mirrors, bar, and stair railings. High ceilings accommodate a mezzanine floor, expanding the seating capacity while adding spatial drama to the main dining room.

Stone surfaces throughout add warmth and refinement, grounding the more polished elements with natural texture. Modern lighting fixtures create a layered illumination strategy, and a selection of green indoor plants introduces freshness against the grey-and-gold scheme. The result is a sophisticated, luxurious atmosphere that impresses without feeling cold.
