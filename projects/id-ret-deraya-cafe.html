Deraya University Cafeteria

Deraya University, New Minya

The Deraya University Cafeteria is an octagonal steel-framed structure with large glass openings that flood the interior with natural light. Light granite tile flooring and a central specimen tree anchor the space with warmth and a touch of nature, while the exposed ceiling with pendant lighting adds an industrial-contemporary character.

Seating is a mix of comfortable loose furniture and fixed tables with stools — providing flexibility for different group sizes and moods. The result is a welcoming, functional space where students can relax, eat, and socialise between classes, with an atmosphere that feels both modern and refreshingly informal.
