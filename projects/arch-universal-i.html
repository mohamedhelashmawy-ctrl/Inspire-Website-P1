Universal HQ I

Sixth of October, Giza

The three-story headquarters is clad in dark blue aluminum panels that reflect the company's identity, contrasting with white-painted structure, glass areas, and sun breakers. Total floor area is 2,550 m² with 850 m² per floor.

The ground floor houses the reception area, showroom, and meeting rooms. The upper floors are dedicated to 500 m² of office space each, with a conference room and exhibition area also on the ground floor. The building includes a cafeteria, lounges, and breakout rooms, and is located in the Sixth of October industrial zone with easy access to transportation.
