Stove Restaurant

Cairo Festival City, New Cairo

A restaurant interior designed to create warmth and intimacy.

Stove is a full-service restaurant located in The Village at Cairo Festival City — one of New Cairo's premier dining and retail destinations. The brief called for an interior that would create a genuine sense of warmth, comfort, and intimacy within a contemporary commercial setting.

The design balances rich material choices with an open, welcoming spatial arrangement. Indoor and outdoor dining zones were carefully integrated to take advantage of the restaurant's elevated position overlooking the festival fountain, creating a memorable dining experience from multiple vantage points.

The menu spans international cuisine — from starters and salads through to grills and seafood — and the interior was designed to support the full breadth of the dining offer, from casual weekday visits to special occasion dinners.
