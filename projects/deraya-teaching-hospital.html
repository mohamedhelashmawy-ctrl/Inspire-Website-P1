Deraya Teaching Hospital

New Minya, Egypt · 2023

A landmark teaching hospital for New Minya.

The Deraya Teaching Hospital stands as one of the most significant healthcare facilities in Upper Egypt — a purpose-built teaching hospital designed to serve the Deraya University medical community and the surrounding region of New Minya.

The architectural language balances clinical functionality with a welcoming environment, using a restrained palette of materials to create spaces that are both highly efficient and calming for patients, staff, and students alike.
