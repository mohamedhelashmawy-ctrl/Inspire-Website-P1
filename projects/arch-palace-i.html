Private Palace I

Sixth of October, Giza

A four-story mansion with basement, ground floor, first floor, and roof, with a total built-up area of 3,300 m². A spa annex building of 220 m² is located in the backyard. The mansion is set within a 12,000 m² land with a large garden and swimming pool.

The exterior is composed of glass and light paint, giving it a sleek and modern look. The interior is decorated with high-end finishes and furnishings, featuring a spacious living room, dining room, kitchen, home office, multiple bedrooms, and a variety of bathrooms. The spa annex includes a sauna, steam room, Jacuzzi, and massage room.
