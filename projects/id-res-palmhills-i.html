Palm Hills Villa I

Palm Hills, Sixth of October

This three-storey villa in Palm Hills features light marble floors in the entrance hall, staircase, and upper corridors. The living area has luxurious wood flooring and cladding, alongside a modern stainless steel staircase with contemporary ceiling design and modern lighting fixtures.

The villa includes two living rooms on the ground and first floors, a guest toilet, a kitchen, a master bedroom, and two bedrooms sharing a bathroom — all overlooking a beautiful open garden. Materials are carefully selected for their quality and warmth, creating an inviting, understated luxury.
