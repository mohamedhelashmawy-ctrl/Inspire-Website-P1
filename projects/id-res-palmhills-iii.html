Palm Hills Villa III

Palm Hills, Sixth of October

This modern standalone villa in Palm Hills is light-coloured, spacious, and designed to invite the outside in. The reception and living area have high ceilings and large windows flooding the space with natural light. A kitchen partially open to the dining area connects through a wood-clad bar — perfect for entertaining. The master bedroom has its own en-suite, while two additional bedrooms share a bathroom.

The villa also features a separate penthouse on the roof level with stunning views. Wood flooring throughout the main spaces adds warmth; stone cladding provides visual texture; modern ceiling designs with pendant lights complete the composition.
