Bin Hamood Office Building

Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

The office building is a 4,700 m², 6-story structure with a basement for car parking and electrical/mechanical services. The ground floor houses a home appliances store and mezzanine retail space. The upper floors are dedicated to office space with meeting rooms and amenities throughout.

Designed in a modern style with clean lines and a minimalist aesthetic, the exterior is clad in glass and steel. The building is highly energy-efficient, featuring solar panels and rainwater harvesting. The ground floor entrance connects to the building core housing two staircases and two elevators, with each upper floor having its own dedicated lobby and restrooms.
