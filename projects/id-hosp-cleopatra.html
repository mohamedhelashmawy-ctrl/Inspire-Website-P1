Cleopatra Nile Cruise Ship

Luxor & Aswan, Egypt

Cleopatra is a floating luxury Nile cruise offering an unparalleled river experience. The vessel's elegant design seamlessly blends contemporary sophistication with the timeless allure of the Nile. Spacious suites, each with a private balcony, frame breathtaking views of the river's scenic banks.

Interiors are finished in exquisite marble and plush carpets, creating an atmosphere of refined luxury. A dynamic fluidity lines concept runs through the design — softening spatial transitions and adding a contemporary layer to the overall aesthetic. The main deck houses the reception, an inviting lounge, a fine-dining restaurant, and a suite collection. The lower deck is dedicated to service operations. The upper deck provides additional suites and a stair lobby.

The rooftop deck is the crown of the experience: private suites, a rejuvenating spa, a swimming pool, and expansive terraces offer panoramic vistas of the Nile in total privacy.
