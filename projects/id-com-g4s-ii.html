G4S Headquarters II

Pyramids Heights, Giza

The second G4S headquarters building continues the company's established design language: red used as a branding accent against grey marble floors and grey carpet, with glass partitions and aluminium frames maintaining an open, transparent feel. Light-coloured furniture keeps the space feeling airy and contemporary.

Private offices for the management team are arranged alongside open workspaces for staff. A reception and waiting area provide a welcoming arrival sequence, while meeting rooms of various sizes and dedicated service rooms complete a fully equipped corporate environment.
