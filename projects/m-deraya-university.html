Deraya University Campus

New Minya, Upper Egypt

A 167,847 m² private university campus in Upper Egypt.

Inspire Architecture & Engineering was appointed to develop the master plan for Deraya University — a major new private university campus in New Minya, Upper Egypt. The 167,847 m² scheme encompasses five faculty buildings, a lecture hall complex, an administrative building, and a series of service blocks, all arranged around a central outdoor student gathering zone.

The campus concept centres on a landscaped student heart — a network of activity zones, shaded walkways, and garden spaces that foster informal interaction and community. An outer ring road with dedicated parking for each faculty ensures efficient vehicular circulation without compromising the pedestrian experience. Buildings are designed to be energy-efficient and sustainable, incorporating natural ventilation and rainwater harvesting. The project is currently under construction and is expected to be fully completed in 2025.
