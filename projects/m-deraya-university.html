Deraya University Campus

New Minya, Upper Egypt

Deraya University is a 167,847 m² private university project in Upper Egypt. Inspire Architecture & Engineering developed the master plan, which includes five different faculty buildings, a lecture hall complex, an administrative building, and several service blocks.

The concept design centres on an outdoor central student gathering zone comprising landscaped areas, activity zones, and walkways. The project is surrounded by an outer ring road with dedicated parking for each faculty building. The detailed architectural designs for all buildings prioritise energy efficiency and sustainability through natural ventilation and rainwater harvesting. Expected full completion: 2025.
