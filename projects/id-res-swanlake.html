Swan Lake North Coast

Palm Hills, North Coast

This modern beach house villa on the North Coast uses light beige marble and wood floors throughout the living areas and bedrooms. A modern glass-railing staircase with creative ceiling design and layered lighting fixtures anchors the vertical sequence of the two-level structure.

The villa comprises a living room connected to a dining area, guest toilet, kitchen, five en-suite bedrooms, and two maid's rooms — a generously scaled coastal retreat overlooking a beautiful lagoon that spreads through the heart of the development.
