Newgiza Villa 03

Palm Hills, Sixth of October

Newgiza Villa 03 is a 200 m² townhouse landscape divided into two distinct areas: a front yard and a back yard. The front yard is anchored by a modern pergola with basalt flooring — a stylish, functional parking area topped by an elegant entrance staircase that leads to the townhouse front door.

The back yard is home to a shaded bar area — the perfect spot to relax and entertain. The space is planted with a selection of local plants and flowers that bring colour, fragrance, and a sense of quiet tranquility. Local marble and basalt materials unite the design, creating a cohesive and sustainable approach to outdoor living.
