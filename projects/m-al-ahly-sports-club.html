Al Ahly Sports Club

New Cairo, Egypt

Inspire Architecture & Engineering participated in the Al Ahly Club competition to design the latest club master plan in New Cairo. The competition was a multi-stage process, and Inspire progressed through each stage until reaching the final presentation to the club board in February 2017. The firm was awarded second place.

The project site covers an area of 176,000 m² and includes most of the major sporting activities offered by the club. The concept design was based on the historical Al Ahly Sporting Club symbolic eagle, which acted as the plan nucleus and central zone surrounded by different sporting activities and social buildings. The design also incorporated rainwater harvesting and solar energy panels.
