Al Ahly Sports Club

New Cairo

A design competition entry for Egypt's most celebrated club.

Inspire Architecture & Engineering submitted a design competition entry for the master planning of the Al Ahly Sports Club in New Cairo — a landmark commission for one of Africa's most storied football clubs and Egypt's premier sporting institution.

The proposal focused on creating a world-class sports and community destination for New Cairo, integrating state-of-the-art sporting facilities with social and recreational amenities. The scheme was designed to serve both elite athletic performance and the everyday needs of club members and the surrounding community, following the firm's core principle that exceptional design should serve everyone.
