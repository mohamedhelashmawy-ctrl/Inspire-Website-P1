Medmark Headquarters

Mohandessin, Giza

The Medmark office draws its colour palette from the company's brand — light blue and grey tones that convey calm and professionalism. The reception features a light blue marble floor and a statement chandelier, while corridors are finished in the same hue with grey carpets and glass partitions that enhance the sense of space.

Open-concept workspaces with floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interior with natural light. Separate manager offices and breakout rooms provide focus and privacy, while a conference room with state-of-the-art AV and a geometric modern ceiling design serves as the centrepiece for team gatherings and presentations.
