City Gate Villa 01

City Gate, New Cairo

This contemporary villa embraces a unique blend of modern design and organic curves. The irregular geometry is softened throughout by sweeping arches in doorways and windows, creating a sense of flow and visual intrigue. The generous ground floor reception area seamlessly opens to the dining space, bathed in natural light, with a guest toilet and a sleek kitchen completing the level.

The central staircase ascends to the first floor, where the living room connects three bedrooms: a master suite with en-suite bathroom and two additional bedrooms sharing a spacious bathroom. Modern functionality meets the timeless elegance of curved forms throughout every space.
