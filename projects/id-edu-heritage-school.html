Heritage International School

Sixth of October, Giza

The Heritage International School Phase 2 extension is a modern, minimal interior design that integrates seamlessly with the existing Phase 1 building. The design prioritises functionality, creativity, and community — organising the building around a central inner courtyard that serves as a vibrant social hub and brings natural light deep into the plan.

The ground floor houses the KG reception and lower school section alongside staff offices. The first and second floors accommodate elementary and secondary classrooms, a library, and a computer lab. The basement features a spacious theatre, foyers, and a large cafeteria that opens directly to the main courtyard.

The architectural language emphasises clean lines, light wood tones, and restrained surfaces. Single-loaded corridors overlooking the courtyard foster a sense of connection — creating a serene, inspiring environment that encourages both academic achievement and personal growth.
