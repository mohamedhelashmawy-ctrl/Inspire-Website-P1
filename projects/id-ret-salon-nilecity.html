Mohamed Elsagheer Salon — Nile City

Fairmont Nile City, Cairo

Located within the Fairmont Nile City hotel, this Mohamed Elsagheer salon brings the brand's signature premium aesthetic to one of Cairo's most prestigious addresses. Light polished marble and brown wood claddings create a warm, elegant atmosphere befitting the five-star setting.

The salon is divided into distinct functional zones — reception, styling area, and waiting lounge — each with its own design character while maintaining a coherent language throughout. A sleek ceiling with elegant lighting units adds a layer of refined sophistication, making the experience of the salon as memorable as the service it delivers.
