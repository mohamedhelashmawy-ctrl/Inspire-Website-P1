Flowtex Headquarters

Sixth of October

The office uses an elegant off-white and brown colour scheme to create a warm, professional atmosphere. An executive zone at the front of the space features large, well-lit offices and meeting rooms finished in dark brown wood — designed to impress clients and senior management alike.

The staff zone at the rear is more relaxed, with varied materials including wood, metal, and glass, and open collaboration areas alongside individual offices. A central reception and waiting area in light beige connects the two zones, welcoming visitors with a comfortable and polished environment.
