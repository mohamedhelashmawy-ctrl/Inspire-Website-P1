Alora Beach Resort

Ain Sokhna, Red Sea

The beach resort is located on a 78,000 m² plot of land in Ain Sokhna with stunning views of the Red Sea. It features a contemporary design with chalets of different sizes, a lagoon, a beachfront, a five-star hotel, and retail areas.

The chalets are built in a variety of styles from traditional Egyptian to modern beachfront — spacious and luxurious with private balconies and patios overlooking the resort amenities. The lagoon is a tranquil oasis at the heart of the resort. The five-star hotel offers restaurants, bars, swimming pools, a spa, and a fitness center.
