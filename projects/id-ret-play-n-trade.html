Play N Trade

City Stars, Cairo

The Play N Trade video game shop at City Stars, Cairo is a bold, brand-driven interior designed for gamers of all ages. Grey, black, and blue — drawn directly from the brand palette — create a sleek, sophisticated atmosphere that feels both modern and immersive.

Walls lined with game shelves, a dominant gaming console display at the centre, and comfortable seating areas for in-store play make the space as functional as it is visually striking. The result is a retail environment that genuinely embodies the brand's identity.
