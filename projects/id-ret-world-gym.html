World Gym

Grey Square Mall, Sixth of October

The World Gym interior takes its cues from the industrial aesthetic: exposed ceilings, marble and rubber flooring, and black steel profiles create an energetic, no-nonsense backdrop for serious training. Light grey walls provide a neutral canvas for the red brand accents, which punctuate the space with energy and identity.

Large windows flood the gym with natural light, making the space feel open and motivating. Creative zoning carves the floor plan into dedicated areas for cardio, strength training, and yoga, each with a clear identity within the larger whole. The gym also features a state-of-the-art fitness centre, sauna, and steam room.
