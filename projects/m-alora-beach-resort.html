Alora Beach Resort

Ain Sokhna, Red Sea · 2020

A 78,000 m² coastal resort on the Red Sea.

The Alora Beach Resort master plan covers 78,000 m² of prime Red Sea coastline in Ain Sokhna, designed for client Alarinova. The scheme combines residential chalets, a five-star hotel, retail areas, and a central lagoon, all unified by a contemporary design language that draws on both traditional Egyptian vernacular and modern beachfront resort typologies.

The chalets — offered in a range of sizes and styles — are arranged to maximise sea views and provide each unit with generous private outdoor space. The lagoon creates a protected water amenity at the heart of the resort, while the hotel provides a full range of facilities including multiple restaurants, bars, swimming pools, a spa, and a fitness centre. Lush landscaping throughout the public realm softens the transition between built elements and the natural beachfront.
