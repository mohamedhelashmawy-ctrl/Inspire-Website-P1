Living Yards Office

New Cairo

The Living Yards office design seamlessly blends modern aesthetics with functional zoning. The Sales Centre, a vibrant hub of activity, features open lounges designed to foster collaboration and creativity, with six private meeting rooms for client consultations. Wood cladding and sleek black lighting profiles give the space a warm, inviting character.

The Administrative Zone prioritises efficiency, centred on a spacious open-plan area surrounded by private offices. Glass partitions with black profiles maintain transparency while allowing privacy where needed — a well-resolved balance that reflects the company's modern, forward-thinking identity.
