Hospital Exterior

Teaching Hospital, New Minya

An exterior identity for a teaching hospital.

The exterior design of the Teaching Hospital in New Minya establishes a strong institutional identity for one of Upper Egypt's major healthcare facilities — a building that communicates confidence and care through its architectural language.

The facade composition uses a rhythm of projecting brise-soleil elements to moderate solar gain while creating visual texture and depth. The entrance sequence — from forecourt to arrival canopy to lobby — was carefully choreographed to make the transition from public street to clinical interior as welcoming as possible.
