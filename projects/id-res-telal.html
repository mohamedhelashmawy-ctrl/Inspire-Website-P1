Beach House Telal

Telal, North Coast

This modern beach house villa on the North Coast embodies a sleek, contemporary design with vibrant pops of colour. The ground floor is designed for communal living: an open-plan living and dining area connects directly to the landscape via large windows and sliding glass doors, maximising natural light and views. The modern kitchen is adjacent to the dining area; a maid's room, driver's room, and guest toilet are conveniently located on the same level.

The first floor is dedicated to private spaces: two en-suite bedrooms offer ultimate comfort, with two additional bedrooms and a shared bathroom for guests or family. Clean lines, minimalist aesthetics, and bold colour accents define the design concept — a vibrant, luxurious atmosphere built on high-quality materials and thoughtful spatial planning.
