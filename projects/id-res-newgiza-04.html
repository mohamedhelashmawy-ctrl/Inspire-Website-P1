Newgiza Villa 04

Newgiza, Sixth of October

This standalone Newgiza villa captivates with its contrast between a neo-classical ground floor and a thoroughly modern upper floor. The vestibule leads to a grand reception adorned with light marble floors and elegant wall panels; the dining and living areas flow seamlessly, bathed in natural light from expansive golf course views.

The first floor is modern in aesthetic: a central living room serves as a communal hub, with the master bedroom enjoying a private en-suite and three additional bedrooms sharing two bathrooms. The basement adds a traditional English court for entertaining and two en-suite rooms — a villa that seamlessly blends timeless elegance with contemporary comfort.
