Jubail Villa IV

Jubail, Sixth of October

This three-level light-coloured contemporary villa opens with an attractive geometric marble floor pattern in the entrance lobby. The reception and dining room feature dark brown herringbone wood flooring with timber cladding and golden ornaments — set against dark green furniture fabrics for a rich, refined contrast. The glass-railing staircase with wooden handles and modern ceiling designs with attractive lighting completes the composition.

The villa comprises three bedrooms, three bathrooms, reception, guest toilet, kitchen, and two living rooms across the ground and first floors — all spacious, well-lit, and defined by clean contemporary lines with natural light throughout.
