Obelisk Nile Towers

Aswan, Egypt

Obelisk Nile Towers is a contemporary hotel on the banks of the Nile in Aswan, blending modern design with traditional Egyptian architectural elements. The hotel comprises a basement, ground floor, mezzanine, and five guest-room floors, crowned by a stunning rooftop with two Jacuzzis and a panoramic terrace.

The ground floor sets the tone with a stylish reception, inviting lounges, and a café. The mezzanine level elevates the dining experience with a sophisticated restaurant and additional lounge areas. The 31 guest rooms — including suites on the fourth floor — are designed for comfort and privacy, with a modern-yet-cosy palette of Aswan granite, HDF panelling, and carpet flooring.

Indirect lighting is used throughout to create a warm, atmospheric ambiance that enhances both the public spaces and guest rooms. The interiors are a carefully crafted balance between local identity and contemporary luxury — a fitting complement to Aswan's extraordinary landscape.
