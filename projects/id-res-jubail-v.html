Jubail Villa V

Jubail, Sixth of October

Jubail Villa V is a three-level light-coloured contemporary structure with golden ornaments. The entrance lobby and first-floor corridor are finished in beige marble, while the reception features luxurious light-coloured herringbone wood flooring. Classical wall panels and a steel-railing staircase with wooden handles anchor the lower levels, while ceilings shift from classical on the ground floor to modern on the upper levels.

The villa comprises a reception, dining room, guest toilet, kitchen, en-suite bedroom, two bedrooms with a shared bathroom, and two living rooms across the first and roof levels. The light, airy colour scheme with gold accents creates a sophisticated contemporary home that balances classical sensibility with modern comfort.
