Palm Hills Villa 06

Palm Hills, Sixth of October

This grand stand-alone contemporary villa in Palm Hills exudes scale and light. The ground floor opens to a high-ceilinged reception area, formal dining room, guest toilet, dedicated office with private bathroom, and an expansive kitchen. A glass house on the ground floor encloses an indoor pool with seating areas, opening directly onto the garden and outdoor pool.

The first floor centres on a large living room with breathtaking golf course views through a grand terrace. An open kitchen and utility area complete the floor. The master suite is generously proportioned with a luxurious bathroom; two additional en-suite bedrooms and a maid's room provide ample accommodation. This villa is the ultimate expression of contemporary luxury living.
