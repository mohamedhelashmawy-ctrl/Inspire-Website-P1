Faculty Courtyard

Deraya University, New Minya

An outdoor gathering space at the heart of campus.

The Faculty Courtyard was designed as the social and spatial heart of the Deraya University faculty cluster — a generous, shaded outdoor space that encourages informal gathering, study, and movement between the various academic buildings.

Careful landscaping, pergola structures, and a considered planting strategy create a microclimate that provides shade and relief from the Egyptian sun, making the courtyard a genuinely usable space year-round.
