Royal Meadows Villa I

Royal Meadows, Sixth of October

A stunning example of eclectic modern-Moroccan architecture, this three-level villa in Sheikh Zayed blends traditional Moroccan elements with contemporary design. The ground floor opens to an entrance lobby and reception, dining room, guest toilet, and kitchen. The first floor houses a master bedroom with private bathroom and dressing room, and two additional bedrooms sharing a bathroom.

The penthouse offers a living room with private kitchenette and a spacious terrace overlooking the green surroundings. Cosy colours, floor lanterns, and wall decorations drawn from Moroccan tradition create a warm and deeply distinctive home.
