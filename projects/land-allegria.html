Allegria Villa

Allegria, Sixth of October

The Allegria Villa garden is a 260 m² private landscape divided into a front yard and a back yard. The front yard features a stone walkway that leads to the house entrance, flanked by colourful planters brimming with flowers and ornamental plants.

The back yard centres on a wooden pergola with a marble floor — a shaded retreat that provides respite from the Egyptian sun. Surrounding planters are filled with lush, colourful plants that bring life and beauty to the space year-round. The design balances functionality and aesthetics, using natural materials such as stone and marble to create a serene, calm atmosphere.
