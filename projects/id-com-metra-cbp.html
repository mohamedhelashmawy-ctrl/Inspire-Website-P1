Metra HQ — Cairo Business Park

Cairo Business Park, Sixth of October

Designed as a modern and sleek open workspace, the Metra Cairo Business Park office features exposed ceilings with contemporary lighting that creates a sense of height and openness. The overall mood is cosy yet efficient, with a palette of neutral tones that lets the spatial design take centre stage.

Private offices provide quiet concentration spaces for management, while open areas encourage collaboration. The layout is flexible — movable partitions allow different configurations as team needs evolve. Essential services including toilets, pantry, IT rooms, and service rooms are fully integrated.
