Universal Headquarters I

Sixth of October

Designed for an electrical appliances company, this modern office is sleek and efficient with a focus on teamwork. Grey porcelain tiles and a minimalist ceiling design create a clean, contemporary look; light wood furniture adds warmth; and dark blue accents echo the company's brand identity.

The office is organised into private offices for management, open workspaces for staff collaboration, and meeting rooms of varying sizes, complemented by a reception area, break room, and copy room. The goal throughout was a productive, motivating environment where employees feel comfortable and engaged.
