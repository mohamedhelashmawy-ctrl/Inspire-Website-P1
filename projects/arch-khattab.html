Khattab Headquarters

Haram, Giza

A 150 m² single-story modern office building featuring stone cladding and curved glass surfaces. The facade also incorporates aluminum cladding sheds and linear glass areas with sun breakers.

The interior features an open-plan layout with a central office area, lounge, and meeting room — designed to be comfortable and productive. The office is fully equipped with all necessary electromechanical services, air conditioning, heating, ventilation, security system, and a backup generator.
