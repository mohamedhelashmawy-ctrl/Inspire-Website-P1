Hacienda White Chalet

Hacienda White, North Coast

This modern beach villa on the North Coast is a two-level, three-bedroom home with a living area, open kitchen, guest toilet, and large terrace on the upper floor. The colour scheme is white with brown woodwork and blue accents — a palette perfectly matched to the coastal setting.

Grey porcelain tile floors, a glass-railing staircase, and sleek modern ceilings create a clean, airy atmosphere throughout. Large windows in the living and dining area offer stunning sea views; each of the four en-suite bedrooms has its own private access to the outdoors. The upper terrace is perfect for sunbathing or relaxing above the water.
