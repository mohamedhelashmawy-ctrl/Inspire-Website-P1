Bar & Lounge

Mövenpick Royal Lotus, Nile Cruise

An intimate evening space on the Nile.

The bar and lounge of the Mövenpick Royal Lotus was designed to create an intimate yet sophisticated evening atmosphere — a place where guests can gather as the Nile scenery drifts past the panoramic windows.

Rich, deep-toned materials — dark timber, soft leather seating, and warm brass accents — contrast with the lighter daytime palette of the adjacent spaces, creating a distinct nocturnal mood appropriate for a premium cruise experience.
