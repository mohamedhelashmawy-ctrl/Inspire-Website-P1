Deraya Faculty of Business

Deraya University, New Minya

The Faculty of Business at Deraya University occupies ground, first, second, and a partial roof level. At its heart lies a grand double-height atrium reception that sets the tone for a modern, inviting learning environment. Lecture rooms are distributed across all three main floors, and the ground level houses a library and computer lab.

The atrium is clad in expansive marble and wood surfaces, creating a sleek contemporary aesthetic that fosters a sense of community among students and faculty. Staff offices are thoughtfully placed throughout the building, with the Dean's suite on the third floor.
