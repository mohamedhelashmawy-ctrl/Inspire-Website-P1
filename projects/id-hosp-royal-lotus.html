Royal Lotus

Luxor & Aswan, Egypt

The Royal Lotus is a touristic Nile cruise ship sailing between Luxor and Aswan. The vessel accommodates a spacious reception connected to a main lounge and restaurant, two upper lounges, 58 standard rooms and two suites, a roof deck, and all associated service spaces.

The design language is contemporary throughout: light wood claddings create warmth and continuity across all decks, while modern ceiling details and integrated wall lighting animate each space with a refined, atmospheric quality. Furniture was selected for both style and comfort — a mix of artistic pieces in varied tones that give the interiors a curated, boutique feel.

The Royal Lotus achieves a balance between modernity and a subtle Egyptian sensibility, creating an environment where passengers can relax, dine, and experience the timeless beauty of the Nile in sophisticated comfort.
