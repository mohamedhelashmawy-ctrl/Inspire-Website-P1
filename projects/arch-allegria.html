Allegria Villa

Allegria, Sheikh Zayed

A modern villa in Allegria, Sheikh Zayed, built on two buildings connected by a bridge on the first floor. The villa has a 4,600 m² built-up area and consists of a basement, ground floor, and first floor, with stunning golf course views.

The basement houses the garage, storage, and utility rooms. The ground floor features an open-concept living and dining area, kitchen, and powder room. The first floor has four bedrooms, each with en suite bathroom. The master bedroom has a private balcony with golf course views. The villa is finished with high-quality materials in a modern design with clean lines and plenty of natural light.
