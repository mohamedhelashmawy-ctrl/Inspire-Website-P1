Faculty of Dentistry

Deraya University, New Minya

A specialist dental faculty for Deraya University.

The Faculty of Dentistry at Deraya University required a highly specialised architectural programme accommodating dental clinics, simulation laboratories, lecture halls, and faculty offices — all designed to meet the stringent technical requirements of dental education.

The building echoes the material palette of the wider Deraya University campus, creating visual continuity while establishing its own distinct identity through the carefully articulated entrance facade and clinical wing organisation.
