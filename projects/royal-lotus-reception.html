Royal Lotus Reception

Mövenpick Royal Lotus, Nile Cruise

A grand arrival experience aboard the Nile.

The reception of the Mövenpick Royal Lotus Nile cruise ship sets the tone for the entire voyage — an elegant, welcoming space that draws on contemporary Egyptian motifs interpreted through a warm, timeless material palette of marble, brass, and deep-toned timber.

Every element was custom-designed for the vessel: from the reception desk detailing to the ceiling feature, lighting scheme, and bespoke furniture — creating a cohesive, five-star arrival experience worthy of one of Egypt's premier Nile cruise ships.
