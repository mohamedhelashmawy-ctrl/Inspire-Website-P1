Jubail Villa I

Jubail, Sixth of October

A contemporary villa in Sixth of October with three levels. The ground floor features an inviting lobby, reception, dining room, guest toilet, and kitchen. The entrance lobby has a creative geometric black-and-white marble floor pattern; reception walls are decorated with intricate colourful artworks, and openings are framed with wooden architraves. Wide ground-floor openings lead to a lovely garden.

The first floor connects a master bedroom with two en-suite bedrooms and a nanny's room via a wide corridor. The penthouse offers an additional living room with private toilet and a large roof terrace. Floors are marble in the lobby, stairs, and corridors, with modern wood planking in the principal rooms. Ceilings are sleek with indirect lighting throughout.
