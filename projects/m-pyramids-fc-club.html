Pyramids FC Club

Sixth of October, Giza

The Pyramids Sports Complex is a state-of-the-art facility covering 196,000 m² and divided into three main zones: the Pyramids FC stadium, the football training facilities, and the social club.

The 40,000-seat Pyramids FC stadium is equipped with a state-of-the-art sound system, high-definition video scoreboard, comfortable seating, and VIP suites. The training facilities include a full-size pitch, training pitch, and gym. The social club features a swimming pool, tennis, basketball, and squash courts, plus a restaurant, bar, and conference room.
