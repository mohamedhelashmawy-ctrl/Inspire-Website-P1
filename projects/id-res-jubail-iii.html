Jubail Villa III

Jubail, Sixth of October

This eclectic standalone villa is a feast for the senses. The ground floor reception is defined by a geometric steel partition and a stunning marble floor pattern. The reception and dining room feature a creative chevron wood floor with high ceilings and large windows overlooking a garden and pool. A central fireplace divides the living area from the salon; a wood-and-glass sliding door separates the reception from the dining room.

The first floor offers a functional living room, kitchenette, master bedroom, and two en-suite bedrooms. A penthouse connects to a large roof terrace with views of the surroundings. The basement is designed in an industrial scheme — office, living room, maid's room, and service spaces — creating a villa that moves fluently between eclectic, modern, and industrial registers.
