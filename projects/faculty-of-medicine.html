Faculty of Medicine

Deraya University, New Minya

The Faculty of Medicine at Deraya University in New Minya is a modern, state-of-the-art facility providing students with the resources they need to succeed in their studies. The building has a footprint of 6,200 m² and a built-up area of 21,850 m² across five levels.

It comprises 10 large lecture halls, a number of labs and classrooms accommodating 200 students per cohort, and all the student support spaces they need — including study rooms, a library, and activity rooms. The building also includes assistant staff offices, department heads' offices, and the dean's section.

The interior is highly modern. The triple-height entrance lobby is clad in wood and marble, featuring an atrium with glass railings. The facade was carefully designed with the environment in mind: large glass areas face away from direct sun, while smaller openings and sun breakers control solar exposure. The facade also includes marble and aluminum cladding, alongside an iconic outdoor shed for gatherings.
