Notion International School

Sixth of October, Giza · 2020

A contemporary school campus designed for 21st-century learning.

The Notion International School is a three-story building with a basement, located on a 14,340 m² plot of land in the Sixth of October district. The basement houses all of the school's utilities and electromechanical spaces, keeping service infrastructure completely clear of learning areas above.

The first and second floors are dedicated entirely to classrooms — 70 rooms in total, serving all educational stages from early years through to secondary. The third floor houses the library, theater, and staff offices, placing shared academic resources at the building's crown where they benefit from the best natural light and views.

The exterior language is simple and geometrical, using warm earth tones that ground the building in its context. Large windows flood every classroom with natural light while a system of horizontal fins provides solar shading. A generous shaded playground wraps the building, giving students safe, comfortable outdoor space during recess throughout the year.

Sustainability was central to the brief: energy-efficient materials, a rainwater collection system for irrigation, and a green roof contribute to the school's low operational footprint. The building is fully wheelchair accessible, with dedicated provisions ensuring equitable access to all facilities for students of all abilities.
