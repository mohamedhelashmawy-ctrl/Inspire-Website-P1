Dubai Apartment

Dubai, UAE

This modern apartment in a Dubai high-rise enjoys stunning views of the Arabian Gulf. The open-plan living and dining area is finished in light beige marble flooring with modern wooden bookshelves creating warmth and definition within the open space. The centrally located kitchen is also open-plan, with stainless steel appliances and quartz countertops.

Both bedrooms are en-suite with modern, practical furnishings. The master bedroom features a private balcony with sea views — a rare luxury that frames the living experience of this internationally situated project.
