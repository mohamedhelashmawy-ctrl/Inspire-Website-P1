Heritage International School

Sixth of October, Giza

The Heritage International School landscape presents a harmonious blend of athletic facilities and communal spaces within a sleek, modern aesthetic. The centrepiece is a 7-a-side natural turf football field, flanked by shaded grandstands that provide comfortable viewing for spectators and create a strong visual anchor to the design.

Adjacent to the football field, a multi-purpose court caters to a range of sports and recreational activities. A half-basketball court and a padel court provide further sporting options. A shaded swimming pool surrounded by gathering areas offers a refreshing retreat, while the site's topography is exploited to create stepped seating areas with dynamic vantage points.

The kindergarten court is designed as a special private zone — colourful, modern, and inviting — fostering a playful environment for young learners. Neutral-coloured materials create a timeless minimalist backdrop throughout, softened by lush planting and integrated water features.
