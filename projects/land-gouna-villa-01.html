Gouna Shedwan Villa 01

Shedwan, El Gouna

This villa landscape in Shedwan, El Gouna seamlessly blends luxury with the captivating desert environment. The centrepiece is an infinity pool that stretches toward the glistening lake, creating a visual harmony between the turquoise water and the endless blue horizon. Submerged chaise lounges within the pool offer a cool, immersive respite.

Three strategically placed wooden pergolas cast dappled shade over poolside lounging areas and an outdoor dining space, their natural tones complementing the desert backdrop. Lush planters, bursting with vibrant desert flora — bougainvillea, aloe vera, native cacti, and succulents — add pops of colour and fragrance. The selection of plants ensures minimal water usage, perfectly suited to the arid climate.

Crushed gravel pathways meander through the meticulously maintained desert landscaping, connecting the various outdoor zones. The result is a tranquil oasis that frames the breathtaking El Gouna views while providing every amenity for relaxed outdoor living.
