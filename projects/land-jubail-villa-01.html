Jubail Villa 01

Jubail, Sixth of October

Jubail Villa 01 is a 265 m² private villa garden featuring a local marble walkway that winds through the garden, leading to a series of terraces. The walkway is punctuated by stepping stones that create a playful, visually interesting rhythm underfoot.

The terraces are home to a variety of planters filled with colourful seasonal flowers and ornamental plants. In one corner, a bar shaded by a pergola provides the perfect spot to relax and entertain — a refined private retreat that balances function and beauty. The use of local marble brings both elegance and sustainability, remaining cool underfoot in the Egyptian heat.
