Palm Hills Villa 06

Palm Hills, Sixth of October

This Palm Hills Golf Views villa is designed around the seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. The centrepiece is a luxurious indoor pool enclosed by a panoramic glass wall — step through and find yourself embraced by an adjoining outdoor pool, creating both a visual and physical dialogue between the two spaces.

Ample shaded areas, positioned with pergolas and awnings, provide respite from the Egyptian sun. Elegant planters cascade with vibrant bougainvillea, fragrant jasmine, and lush ferns, adding texture and colour against the backdrop of the golf course panorama. The overall landscaping complements the desert environment with drought-resistant species: olive trees, date palms, and other low-water Mediterranean plants that thrive without heavy maintenance.

The result is a meticulously crafted private oasis — blurring the boundaries between the luxurious interior and the captivating golf course views.
