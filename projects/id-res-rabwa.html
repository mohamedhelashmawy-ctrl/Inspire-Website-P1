Rabwa Villa

Jubail, Sixth of October

This two-storey contemporary villa in Sheikh Zayed is spacious and luxurious. The ground floor features an entrance lobby and reception, guest toilet, kitchen, and private office. A beautiful circular wood-clad staircase is the centrepiece of the vertical circulation. Reception walls are covered in rich wood cladding; the dining room has stunning antique glass wall panels. Wide ground-floor openings frame views of a lovely garden and swimming pool.

The first floor has a spacious central living area connected to a kitchenette, surrounded by four en-suite bedrooms — including a master bedroom with large dressing room and private bathroom. Ceiling designs shift from classical with indirect lighting on the ground floor to more modern on the first, creating a rich sense of progression through the villa.
