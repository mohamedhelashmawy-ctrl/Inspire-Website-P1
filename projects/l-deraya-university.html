Deraya University Landscape

New Minya, Upper Egypt

Campus-scale landscape design for Deraya University.

The landscape design for Deraya University is an integral component of the wider campus master plan — a carefully considered open-space strategy that creates a cohesive, green, and human-scaled environment across the 167,847 m² site.

The design centres on a generous outdoor student gathering zone at the heart of the campus, featuring landscaped lawns, shaded walkways, planting beds, and flexible activity zones. Winding pedestrian routes connect the five faculty buildings and the lecture hall complex, encouraging movement and social interaction. The planting palette is adapted to the Upper Egyptian climate, prioritising shade, drought-tolerance, and low maintenance without compromising visual quality.
