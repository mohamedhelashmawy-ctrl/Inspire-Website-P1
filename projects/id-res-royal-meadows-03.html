Royal Meadows Villa 03

Royal Meadows, Sixth of October

This two-storey residence embraces a classic architectural style with timber cornices, wall frames, and traditional architectural details throughout. The entrance vestibule features marble-patterned flooring leading to a wood-panelled reception area, from which a grand wooden staircase ascends to the first floor.

The ground floor accommodates a spacious lobby, a formal reception room with dining table, a well-appointed kitchen, and guest toilet. The first floor houses the master bedroom with en-suite bathroom, two additional bedrooms, and a comfortable living room — a timeless and sophisticated home that draws its character from classical proportion and material quality.
