Allegria Villa I

Allegria, Sixth of October

This modern villa in Allegria is designed with clean lines and an emphasis on natural light. The ground floor features an entrance lobby and reception, dining room, guest toilet, kitchen, and a private office. An iconic staircase with vertical decorative elements connects to the first floor, which houses the master bedroom with its own bathroom and two additional bedrooms.

The penthouse level features a living room opening to a private terrace with views over the surroundings. Throughout, the palette is ultra-modern: white marble flooring across most spaces, light grey wood in the bedrooms, and sleek ceiling designs with contemporary lighting.
