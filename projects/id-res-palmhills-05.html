Palm Hills Villa 05

Palm Hills Golf Views, Sixth of October

This modern townhouse in Palm Hills Golf Views blends seamlessly with its natural surroundings. The reception and dining area feature light beige marble flooring; living areas and bedrooms are finished with warm wood floors. A modern glass-railing staircase connects the levels, with indirect lighting and contemporary fixtures throughout the ceilings.

The townhouse comprises three en-suite bedrooms, two living rooms, a guest toilet, kitchen, maid's room, and a utility area at roof level — a well-resolved plan that balances openness with privacy in a Golf Views setting.
