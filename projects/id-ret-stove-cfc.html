Stove Restaurant — CFC

Cairo Festival City, New Cairo

The Stove CFC interior is a study in restrained luxury. Grey dominates the palette, with golden profiles appearing in the shelving, wall ornaments, and glasswork — adding warmth and glamour without ostentation. High ceilings and marble cladding lend the space a sense of occasion, while modern lighting fixtures maintain an intimate dining atmosphere.

A splendid central tree anchors the dining room, bringing a touch of nature into the refined interior. The restaurant also features a large terrace with views over an artificial lake and fountain — an exceptional setting for al-fresco dining on Cairo's warmer evenings.

The Stove brand's design approach — grey foundations, gold accents, and curated greenery — is executed here with a level of spatial quality that sets it apart within the Cairo dining scene.
