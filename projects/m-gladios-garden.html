Gladios Garden

Sixth of October, Giza

A residential compound master plan with views of the Pyramids.

Gladios Garden is a multi-phase residential compound master plan located in Sixth of October, Giza, benefiting from stunning direct views of the Pyramids of Giza. Inspire Architecture & Engineering was responsible for the master planning of the compound, which offers a diverse range of residential unit types — from compact apartments through to generously proportioned stand-alone villas and family houses.

The master plan is structured as three phases, each with its own distinct residential character and landscape identity, while maintaining visual and functional coherence across the whole scheme. Central landscaped areas, communal amenities, and a considered circulation network create a cohesive community environment. The scheme's elevated positioning towards the Pyramids provides every resident with one of the most iconic views in Egypt.
