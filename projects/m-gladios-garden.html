Gladios Garden

Sixth of October, Giza

The Pyramid View Residential Complex is a 23,888 m² development consisting of 12 apartment blocks, all with stunning views of the Giza Pyramids. The complex is located in a prime location just minutes from the Pyramids and the Sphinx.

The complex features a central lagoon with fountains and seating areas, landscaped gardens with walking paths and open spaces, a swimming pool and sun terrace, gym and spa, a children's play area, a business centre, and 24-hour security. Apartment blocks range from one-bedroom to four-bedroom units, all with balconies or terraces overlooking the pyramids.
