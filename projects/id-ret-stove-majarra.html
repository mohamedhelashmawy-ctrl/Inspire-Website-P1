Stove Restaurant — Majarra

Majarra Mall, Sixth of October

The Stove Majarra interior is a warm, cosy take on the brand's signature grey-and-gold palette. Red brick is introduced here as a grounding material — its texture contrasting with the reflective surfaces of mirrored golden lines and the bar finishes. Suspended decorative elements and hanging plants add depth and visual interest to the ceiling plane.

Modern lighting fixtures cast an interesting, layered light across the dining room, while the mix of green indoor plants against the grey, gold, and red scheme delivers an unexpected sense of elegance. The overall atmosphere is relaxed but polished — perfectly pitched for a neighbourhood dining destination.
