Dyar Villa

Dyar, New Cairo

This contemporary three-storey villa in Dyar, New Cairo has a spacious entrance lobby and reception, dining room, guest toilet, and kitchen. Reception walls are covered in rich wood cladding, with all openings clad in wooden architraves. Wide ground-floor openings open to a lovely garden and outdoor jacuzzi. The ground floor has a classical-contemporary character; the first floor is more modern in execution.

The first floor centres on a spacious living area connected to an open kitchen, surrounded by a master bedroom with large dressing room and private bathroom, and two en-suite bedrooms. The penthouse offers an additional living room, private toilet, and large roof terrace with views of the surroundings. Floors are predominantly marble with a stunning entrance lobby pattern; bedrooms and living rooms have warm wood flooring.
