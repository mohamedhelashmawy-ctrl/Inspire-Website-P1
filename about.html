Our Story

2010
Founded in Cairo

Architecture as a vital right.

Inspire Architecture & Engineering was founded in Cairo by Architect Hassan Elashmawy, a 2005 graduate of Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering who honed his craft at the world-renowned firm Dar Al Handasah.

Driven by the belief that proper architecture is not a luxury but a fundamental right, Inspire was built on the principle that everyone deserves to inhabit spaces that are functional, beautiful, and deeply considered.

From our base in Cairo we have grown to deliver projects across Egypt — hospitals, universities, cruise ships, schools — each one approached with the same rigour and commitment to craft that has defined the practice from its first day.

The Founder

Architect Hassan Elashmawy

Hassan Elashmawy graduated from Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering in 2005, where he developed a rigorous foundation in architectural theory and technical practice.

Following graduation, he joined Dar Al Handasah — one of the world's leading engineering and architecture consultancies — where he was involved in large-scale projects across the Middle East and North Africa, building a breadth of expertise that would later define Inspire's approach to complex briefs.

In 2010, he founded Inspire Architecture & Engineering in Cairo with a clear conviction: that the built environment should serve everyone, and that exceptional design quality should not be the preserve of an elite few.
