Who We Are

Built on belief,
driven by design.

Inspire Architecture & Engineering was founded in Cairo in 2010 by Architect Hassan Elashmawy — a 2005 graduate of Cairo University who honed his craft at the world-renowned firm Dar Al Handasah before striking out with a singular vision.

That vision: that proper architecture is not a luxury but a fundamental right. That the spaces people inhabit every day — hospitals, schools, homes, workplaces — profoundly shape their lives, and deserve to be designed with rigour, creativity, and care.

Fifteen years and 200+ projects later, that belief remains the foundation of everything we do.

What We Stand For

Our values &
approach.

Vision
Proper architecture is a vital right. Creativity, practicality and appropriateness create strong relationships between people and the spaces they use every day. We design for that relationship.
Mission
To create spaces of distinction and long-lasting value — adding a signature design identity to every project we touch, whether a single-family villa or a university campus.
Process
Seamless execution from concept through to construction supervision. Every detail overseen with precision, every decision made with purpose. We don't just design spaces — we deliver them.
People
A talented multidisciplinary team combining deep technical knowledge with genuine passion for exceptional design. Architects, interior designers, landscape architects and engineers working as one.
How We Work

Our Process

01
Discovery & Brief
We begin with deep listening — understanding your vision, needs, site constraints and budget to form a precise project brief that guides everything that follows.
02
Concept Design
From the brief we develop spatial concepts, precedent studies and initial design options — exploring possibilities before committing to a direction.
03
Design Development
The chosen concept is refined through detailed drawings, material specifications, engineering coordination and regulatory submissions.
04
Construction & Delivery
We supervise construction from groundbreaking to handover — ensuring every detail is executed with the precision the design demands.
Leadership

Meet the founder.

Hassan Elashmawy
Founder & Principal Architect

A 2005 graduate of Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering (Architecture), Hassan began his professional career at Dar Al Handasah — one of the world's most respected architecture and engineering firms — where he developed the technical depth and design rigour that would define Inspire's approach.

In 2010 he founded Inspire Architecture & Engineering with a conviction that every person deserves to live and work in a thoughtfully designed space. His leadership has guided the firm through 200+ projects across Egypt, from large-scale university campuses to intimate villa landscapes.
