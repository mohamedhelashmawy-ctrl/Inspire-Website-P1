Application Process

How to apply.

01
Send Your CV
Email your CV and a brief cover letter to info@inspirearchitecture.net with the role you're applying for in the subject line.
02
Share Your Portfolio
Include a portfolio PDF (max 10MB) or a link to your work online. Show us the projects you're most proud of and explain your role in each.
03
Interview & Studio Visit
Shortlisted candidates will be invited to the studio in Dokki for a conversation about their work, our projects, and how we can grow together.
