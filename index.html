Our Story

Inspire Architecture & Engineering was founded in Cairo by Architect Hassan Elashmawy, a 2005 graduate of Cairo University who honed his craft at the world-renowned firm Dar Al Handasah. Driven by the belief that proper architecture is not a luxury but a fundamental right, we build meaningful relationships between people and the spaces they inhabit every day.

Our Vision

Proper architecture is a vital right. Creativity, practicality and appropriateness create strong relationships between people and space.

Our Mission

To create spaces of distinction and long-lasting value, adding a signature design identity to every project we touch.

Our Process

Seamless execution from concept through to construction supervision — every detail overseen with precision.

Our People

A talented multidisciplinary team combining deep technical knowledge with genuine passion for exceptional design.
